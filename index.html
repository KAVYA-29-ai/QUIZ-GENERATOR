About QuizGen

QuizGen generates MCQs, True/False, and short-answer questions from uploaded files or pasted text. It uses Google Gemini as primary, Hugging Face as fallback, and an internal fallback to guarantee results.

  • Adaptive difficulty: correct streak increases difficulty automatically in quiz mode.
  • Summarize & explain: press Generate with "Short" type or ask in Chat.
  • Export: download questions as TXT or PDF.

Tech: static frontend (PDF.js client extraction), Netlify Functions for LLM calls (Gemini/HF/fallback). No DB required.

Deploy to Netlify — set GEMINI_API_KEY/GEMINI_MODEL and/or HF_API_KEY/HF_MODEL in site env vars.
